Hi, nice to meet you! I’m Amna, a computer engineer turned digital experience designer with nearly four years of professional experience across research, technology, and startup environments. I previously worked at CERN, where I contributed to the design of complex industrial systems, and I am currently working at Logitech as an AI Creative & Design Technologist. I am also pursuing a Master of Arts in Design with a focus on Digital Experience Design at École cantonale d’art de Lausanne (ECAL).

UI Design

My design practice sits between visual expression and digital experience. I design digital experiences with a soft spot for details: movement, rhythm, layout, and tiny interactions that make a screen feel alive. For me, design is not just about making things look good, it is about making technology feel more human.
