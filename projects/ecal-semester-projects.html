1- Modular Mindset - Animation for music label

As part of a semester workshop, I was tasked with creating a modular, animated visual identity for a fictional music label, exploring the intersection of motion design and procedural systems. Using Cavalry, I developed dynamic visual systems that evolve according to a set of precise rules while maintaining graphic coherence and a strong connection to the label’s sonic universe. The objective was to create a flexible identity capable of adapting seamlessly across digital interfaces and scales, from smartphone screens to large-format billboards.

2- Beyond Clicks - The mind reader Interface

For this project, we were tasked with creating an interactive interface through the lens of Intent-Based Interaction, employing Environmental Awareness to detect a situation or Intent Focus to track visual attention. The interaction model followed a three-phase workflow:

  • Detection of an implicit trigger
  • Affordance where the UI reconfigures to match the tool
  • Progression to predict the next logical step.

Question I asked myself:

What happens when we create technology to solve the social problems we are too polite to address ourselves?

Concept:

A conversation assistant that detects imbalance in social interactions and gently guides people toward more balanced conversations, without taking control away from them.

The focus is on three kind of people in a group scenario:
1- One who talks a lot.
2- One who does not participate in the conversation.
3- One who who is a balanced communicator.

How it works?

Detection:

Speaking imbalance in the room is detected automatically from audio, without explicit user input.

Affordance:

The phone UI reconfigures by cue type vibration only, then full notification with sound.

Progression:

Cues escalate in a fixed sequence (early nudge → stronger notification)
