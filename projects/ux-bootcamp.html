Participatory Design - ECAL Admission Experience

Rethinking one specific part of ECAL’s admission experience by designing with people who are part of it such as Professors, Teacher assistants, Jury members, Students, IT staff and Admin staff - “Designing with people, not only for them”

Roles and Responsibilities:

This workshop was a team effort with Thomas, Gwen and Shinyoung. We designed the workshop plan as a group, and during the session we each took on a clear role. Thomas, Shinyoung and I facilitated the activities and guided participants, while Gwen observed and captured notes so we wouldn’t miss any insights.
