Randox | Certifly mobile app

The Randox Mobile App was developed to simplify and streamline the process of COVID-19 testing during the pandemic. By enabling users to scan a QR code on their testing device, the app provided a seamless and user-friendly experience for health monitoring. This project was a collaborative effort between a UK-based company and my company in Pakistan.

Roles and Responsibilities:

My Role As a Junior Designer, I worked alongside an experienced team of designers and developers based in the UK.
My responsibilities included

  • Maintaining Existing Designs: Ensuring the continuity and quality of the initial design framework.
  • Design Tweaks: Refining UI elements and improving the overall user experience based on feedback and evolving requirements.
  • New Feature Design: Contributing to the creation and integration of new features while maintaining visual and functional consistency.

This role required close collaboration and attention to detail to meet the standards of such a high-stakes project.
