Reality Check - A new Duolingo

Duolingo positions itself as a free, fun, and effective way to learn languages; however, this project challenges that narrative by examining how its design decisions, course structure, and marketing strategies shape users’ learning experiences and motivation. Through a human-centered design lens, I explore how Duolingo can better support diverse learning goals, complement traditional language education, and bridge digital learning with real-world language use.

Research Question:

How do Duolingo’s design, courses and marketing strategies effect users learning experience and motivation, and how can human-centered design interventions can improve it?

Sub Question:

I- What is the motivation of users behind choosing duolingo as a source (primary or secondary) of learning language?
II- How do duolingo compliments the other (more traditional) methods of language learning?
III- How do users perceive the depth and effectiveness of Duolingo’s lessons, in terms of grammar and structured language learning?

Data Gathering:

To build a deep understanding of user behaviors, motivations, and pain points, I employed two complementary research methods:

I- Digital Ethnography
II- Semi-Structured Interviews

I- Digital Ethnography

Digital ethnography was conducted to understand how users naturally discuss, review, critique, and experience the product in real-world contexts.

Sources Explored:

To ensure diverse perspectives, I analyzed multiple digital touchpoints:

  • User reviews on the Google Play Store
  • Online discussion forums
  • Relevant research papers and academic publications
  • Threads on Reddit
  • Video reviews and user walkthroughs on YouTube

II- Semi-Structured Interviews

To complement large-scale digital observations, I conducted semi-structured interviews with 10 participants.

Participant Breakdown

  • 5 Long-Term Users
    Individuals who have consistently used the product over an extended period.
  • 5 Short-Term Users (Drop-offs)
  • Individuals who initially adopted the product but discontinued usage after some time.

This segmentation allowed comparison between sustained engagement and drop-off behaviors.

Data Analysis:

Thematic Analysis

The analysis followed a structured yet iterative approach consisting of three key stages:
I- Coding
II- Pattern Formation
III-Insight Generation
Each stage informed and refined the next.

Users experience Duolingo as isolated, repetitive, and detached from real-world learning contexts, even though language learning is inherently social and contextual.

They feel motivated by Duolingo’s gamification but frustrated by lack of control especially when they want to focus on grammar, vocabulary, or writing.

Insight 1Easy to Start, Hard to Trust
Insight 2Going pro in game, not the language
Insight 3Motivation built on emotions
Insight 4Erosion of Trust because of AI

How might Duolingo evolve from a one-size-fits-all, gamified learning app into a flexible, human-centered learning ecosystem that supports users’ diverse motivations, learning goals, and real-world language use?

User Persona’s: Based on learning motivation

Design Proposal

1- Understanding users, their motivation and needs.

2- Putting user in control as they are motivated but frustrated by lack of control!

3- Since users felt detached from the real world- adding a touch of real life scenarios.

4- Language learning is social, but Duolingo feels solitary.

5- Physical + Digital bridging
Use Duolingo along with traditional means of learning

Conclusion:

Duolingo stands today as one of the most commercially successful and feature-rich language learning applications. My focus was not to add more features, but to question whether all of these existing features truly support the user’s learning experience. Based on the insights gathered, I proposed three design directions that has the potential to be developed further and expanded into something much larger.

However, language learning will always involve more than just an app. It requires real human interaction, motivation, and a genuine love to learn language and culture.
